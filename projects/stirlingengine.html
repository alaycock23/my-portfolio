Stirling Engine Helicopter

Note: This page is a placeholder for a full description page which I am working on implementing.

Below is a video of my Stirling Engine running on solar energy. I harvested a fresnel-lenticular lens from the back of an old rear projection TV and housed it in a custom built rotating frame. I then angled the lens to concentrate sunlight on the air chamber, and the engine operated at 300 RPM.
